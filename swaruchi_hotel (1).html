Our Story

Taste the Soul of
South India

Founded in 2025, Hotel Swaruchi is a celebration of rich South Indian culinary heritage. Every dish is crafted with hand-ground spices, traditional recipes passed down through generations, and a deep love for authentic flavour.


Located in the heart of Kistareddypet, Ameenpur, we serve the community with warmth, freshness, and pride. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian lovers are both welcomed with equal love.
